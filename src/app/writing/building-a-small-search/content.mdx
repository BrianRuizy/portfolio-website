This sample post walks through a small product feature: search. The goal is to show how a focused engineering write-up can explain the user problem, the implementation, and the tradeoffs without turning into a full technical manual.

## Start with the job

Before choosing a library or writing an index, decide what people need to find and how quickly they expect an answer. A small, well-scoped search often benefits more from clear labels and helpful empty states than from a complex ranking system.

## Keep the first version simple

For a compact collection, filter data on the client and make the interaction keyboard-friendly. If the collection grows, the same interface can move to a server-backed search without changing how it feels to use.
