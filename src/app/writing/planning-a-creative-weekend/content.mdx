This sample post is about making creative work easier to finish. A repeatable process can leave more room for the part that matters: making something worth sharing.

## Capture ideas as they arrive

Keep a short list of ideas with one sentence about why each one might be useful or interesting. When it is time to make something, choose an idea that still feels relevant instead of starting from a blank page.

## Give each step its own space

Separate outlining, recording, editing, and publishing into small sessions. A simple checklist helps each session end with a clear next step, even when the project takes a few days.

Use this sample as a base for writing about your own creative process.
