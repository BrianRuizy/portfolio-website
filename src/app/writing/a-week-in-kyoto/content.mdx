This sample life update follows a week spent walking through Kyoto. The best moments were often the quiet ones: a side street before breakfast, a small shop discovered by accident, or a long walk with nowhere specific to be.

## Leave room in the itinerary

It is tempting to plan every stop. Leaving an open afternoon made it easier to follow a street that looked interesting and spend time somewhere that was not on the list.

## Bring a camera, then put it away

Photos help preserve the details, but not every moment needs to become a frame. I tried to take a few pictures, then enjoy the rest of the walk without looking at a screen.
